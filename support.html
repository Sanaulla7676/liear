Support FAQ

Useful answers before you call.

What should I send for a faster diagnosis?

Share the product name, a short description of the issue and a clear photo or video when the problem is visible on the device or screen.

Can you help with software and hardware together?

Yes. Describe the full counter workflow so the issue can be approached as a connected setup rather than as unrelated devices.

Do you help with installation and training?

Installation guidance and practical training support are part of the service workflow where applicable to the selected solution.
